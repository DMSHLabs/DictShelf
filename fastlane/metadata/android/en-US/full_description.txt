wuDict searches all your MDict, StarDict, AARD2, Lingvo DSL, Babylon and ZIM dictionaries on your device.

The app runs the wudict server locally and shows definitions in an embedded WebView. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked,
there is no sign-in — the app works fully offline.

SUPPORTED FORMATS

- MDict (.mdx/.mdd)
- StarDict (.ifo/.idx/.dict)
- Aard 2 (.slob)
- Lingvo DSL (.dsl)
- Babylon (.bgl)
- ZIM (.zim) - library.kiwix.org

Pictures, audio pronunciation and the dictionary's own styles render the
way the dictionary authors intended.

SEARCH

Four ways to find a word up:

- Prefix — all entries starting with your search term
- Exact — just the headword itself
- Contains — any text anywhere in a headword
- Full text — the word or phrase anywhere in an article

All dictionaries are searched concurrently, with results displayed live as they become available.

BUILT FOR LARGE COLLECTIONS

Dictionaries are searchable immediately after adding them. wudict
builds a compact headword index in the background, after which lookups are
almost instant even across hundreds of dictionaries. Preparation is
automatic and needs no configuration; contains-search, full-text search and
media packing are per-dictionary switches you can turn on only for the dictionaries
that you select — these will take extra space with the estimated size displayed in the dictionary panel.

FEATURES
- Long tap on a URL to a dictionary (.mdx/.slob/.dsl, etc) or a .zip/.7z and pick Share to wuDict to install it
- Apply custom CSS styles and custom fonts via the embedded Styler ☰ → ⚙️ → Custom Style

PRIVACY

- No account, no cloud, no telemetry
- No advertising
- The only network permission is used to reach the server running inside the
  app itself, on 127.0.0.1
- This build declares no storage permission at all: dictionaries are imported
  through the system file picker into the app's own storage
