# DictShelf

One executable that turns the dictionaries you already own into a single search
in your browser. DictShelf indexes them on your own machine and answers for all
of them at once: MDict (`.mdx`/`.mdd`), StarDict, Aard 2, Lingvo DSL, Babylon,
ZIM and wudict markdown. Windows, macOS, Linux and Android — no account, no
upload, nothing sent anywhere.
